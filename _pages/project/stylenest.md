## Overview

StyleNest is an innovative app designed for seamless closet organization and outfit planning, catering to users who want to make their morning routines more efficient and carefree. By creating a "digital wardrobe," users can photograph their clothing items and categorize them using customizable tags such as occasion, weather, and style preference. This personalized approach enables users to effectively  assemble outfits that align with their mood, activities, and external conditions.

## Technologies Used

- Javascript
