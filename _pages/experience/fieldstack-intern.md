## About FieldStack

FieldStack is a cloud-based retail operations platform that unified POS, e-commerce, inventory, analytics, and automation to help retailers streamline operations.

## My Role

As a Software Development Intern, I worked as a full-stack developer on an engineering team of eight with a legacy system and shipped multiple features that reached production. I picked up tickets independently and proactively would search for more work when I finished. Technically, I worked primarily in WinForms, C#, and SQL and was exposed to Blazor, HTML, and CSS.

## What I Worked On

**Customer Address-Blocking Feature**
Designed and developed a feature that allows businesses to block customer's addresses
from being used for same-day shipping, improving delivery driver safety. Architected the database and designed the management UI.

---

**Quick Access Menu**
Co-led development of a Quick Access Menu for retail stores to streamline item checkout for store staff. Designed the store management system and database architecture.

---

**Self-Directed Contributions**
Proactively identified and self-assigned development tickets outside the
team's backlog during downtime. This includes a feature allowing clients to change customer icons among other smaller features.
